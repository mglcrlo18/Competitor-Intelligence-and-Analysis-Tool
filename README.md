# Universal Competitor Intelligence Radar

An open, industry-agnostic competitive surveillance and strategic decision-support engine. Built for competitive intelligence specialists, product marketing managers, and executive strategy teams across any commercial vertical (Enterprise SaaS, FinTech, Healthcare, E-Commerce, Manufacturing, Professional Services, and Consumer Goods).

---

## Key Capabilities

1. **Company Profile & Strategic Brief Management:**
   * Input your enterprise identity, core value proposition, key offerings, and competitive differentiators.
   * Dynamically aligns all analytical algorithms and battlecards against your specific company baseline.

2. **Competitor Placement Hub:**
   * Place direct, indirect, incumbent, and disruptor competitors into your active surveillance roster.
   * Define rival pricing hooks, vulnerability friction rates (0-100%), frontline rebuttals, and buyer landmines.

3. **Quantitative Risk & Threat Scoring:**
   * Inherent vs. Residual threat assessment with Downside VaR modeling.
   * Key Competitive Indicators (KCIs) for real-time monitoring of rival pricing pressure and leadership movements.

4. **Frontline Sales Battlecards:**
   * Instant side-by-side objection handling playbooks.
   * Fact-checked claim-versus-reality teardowns and buyer trap questions.

5. **2D Threat-Friction Matrix:**
   * Visual quadrant mapping categorizing rivals into **Market Dominators**, **Vulnerable Giants**, **Marginal Players**, and **Stable Niche Competitors**.

6. **Executive 1-Page Briefings:**
   * Automated C-suite memos dispatched via silent headless SMTP with multi-recipient CC routing.

## Quick Launch

### 1. Launch via Terminal
```bash
cd /Users/macbook/Desktop/universal_competitor_intelligence
./run.sh
```

The application will open in your default browser at `http://localhost:8503`.

---

## Architecture

* **UI Layer:** Streamlit with clean executive typography (Montserrat)
* **Storage Layer:** Standalone SQLite database (`market_radar.db`)
* **Visualization:** Altair interactive charts and responsive executive metrics
* **Delivery:** Headless Python SMTP over secure TLS
